## Decision

- **P1. Proportion.** The org exists for too-big-for-one-context, independent-checking. The single strong agent stays an arm of every run: B8-B10 ran one strong agent (V); it is kept as an arm: V won on cost twice, the org won on correctness (B10's hidden gate).
- **P7. Few members.** The standing crew is architect, w-measure, w-bold, w-lean. w-sceptic are parked. B13: eight members cost $128 a hidden point, against four members' $55. Members were kept by kept credit and points per dollar. A parked member comes back only with a written reason for a fifth.
- **P8. Cost target.** $10 per 0.01 of the instruction ratio. Work over 2× the target pauses for the owner.
- **P10. Predictions.** Every open milestone has predictions, each with a wrong-if (`company/milestones.json`).
- **P6. The referee.** `node referee/judge.mjs fast --dir=.`; its held-out part is hidden W1-W5 tests and SQLite workloads, held by Meta (referees/).
- **P13. Deploys.** Every deploy needs the owner's approval.

## Objections

- *"Parking members loses areas they knew."* Their lessons stay in the knowledge base, and a kept member can read them (P9). Stands until a run shows an area regressing for want of its member.
